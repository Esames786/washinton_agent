Email of 12 October

The website

Seven points: the agent programme, a shorter application, clearer wording, a blog, and a proper quote form for every service.

Agent programme on the home page

Point 1 Open the home page →
Before

Nothing on the home page pointed people to the agent programme. The header offered Login and Signup to every visitor.

Today

A band partway down the home page introduces the programme and links to it with See the Program. The header shows Earn With Us instead of Login/Signup.

Earn With Hello Transport page

Point 2 Open the Earn page →
Before
No page existed. Agents went straight to a signup form with no explanation of the role, the pay or the process.
Today

One page with six sections: overview, why work with us, how to become an agent (six steps), apply, sign in for existing agents, and FAQs.

Apply for an Opportunity

Point 3 Open the application →
Before

A long account form: father and mother names, date of birth, gender, marital status, ID, full address and a password, before anyone had reviewed the person.

Today

The short form from the email: name, email, phone, country, city, timezone, two U.S. questions, optional CV and LinkedIn, and the terms. Choosing a country fills in the phone code and timezone, and the applicant can still change both.

After applying

Point 4
Before

The form created a portal account on the spot and said so.

Today

No account is created. The applicant sees your wording, and the application goes to the HR team for review.

Application SubmittedThank you for applying to join our team. Your application has been successfully received and is now under review. Our recruitment team will review your application and contact you if your profile matches an available opportunity.

Sign-in page wording

Point 5 Open sign-in →
Before

Customer-style wording, marked a to d in the email.

Today

Written for agents: Access Your Account, what the workspace is for, and New to the Hello Transport network? Create Account.

Blog

Point 6 Open the blog →
Before
No blog.
Today

A blog with categories and 10 original articles: car shipping, open vs enclosed, heavy equipment, boats, RVs, LTL vs FTL freight, reefer, RoRo, a vehicle preparation checklist, and the agent role.

Each article carries search-engine fields (title, description, image text, schema), as on ShipA1. Admins and managers write and schedule posts in the portal under Blog Posts.

A quote form for every service

Point 7a Open a heavy equipment quote →
Before

Every service page, boat included, opened the same car form.

Today

Each service opens its own form with ShipA1's fields: heavy equipment (size, weight, trailer, loading method, auction, photo), boat, RV, freight (LTL/FTL, freight class with a calculator, equipment, pickup and delivery services) and RoRo (destination country and city). The details reach the order in the CRM.

Recruitment process

Everything is now managed step by step in the HR portal. The CRM account is created at the end, once the person has been through every step.

Before

Signup created the account

  1. Signup formLong form with a password.
  2. CRM account created immediately"Pending admin approval".
  3. Admin approvesNDA, documents and contract existed, but not as one ordered process.
Today

Application to active agent

  1. Application EMAIL 1Short form on the website. No account yet.
  2. Campaign LeadsNew folder in HR: Pending, Under Review, Shortlisted, Rejected.
  3. Shortlisted EMAIL 2HR profile created; the agent gets a link to set their password.
  4. Not selected EMAIL 3Rejection email with the reason.
  5. NDA EMAIL 4Signed in the HR portal.
  6. Questionnaire EMAIL 4Questions written by the admin in settings.
  7. In progressInterview, practical test and offer. The agent's screens are locked meanwhile.
  8. DocumentsWorldwide document set, below.
  9. Verification and management approval EMAIL 5
  10. Contract EMAIL 4Signed and accepted in the portal.
  11. Onboarding EMAIL 6CRM account created and the sign-in link emailed.
  12. Active
EmailWhen it is sentWhat it contains
1Right after applyingConfirmation that the application was received
2ShortlistedHR portal link and a link to set a password (no password is ever emailed)
3Not selectedA polite rejection with the reason
4NDA, questionnaire or contract is readyA link straight to that step
5Moved to management approvalA status update
6CRM account createdThe CRM sign-in link

Admins can still create an employee directly in HR when they need to; that path is unchanged.

Questionnaire questions

The questionnaire step is built, but no questions have been entered yet. Until there are some, shortlisted agents stop at that step. An admin adds them in the HR portal under Settings → Onboarding Questionnaire.

Needs a decision

Device restriction (item 11)

Proposed rule: the CRM records each device an agent signs in from, and the admin sets a policy per agent: Off (default), One device or Approved devices only. A new device asks for admin approval instead of signing straight in.
